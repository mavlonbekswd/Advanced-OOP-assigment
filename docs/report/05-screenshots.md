The figures below were captured from the running application by driving it through
its own buttons.

**Figure 1 — A new game.** All hundred cells unexplored; the status line reads no
shots, no hits, six ships. Nothing distinguishes a cell holding a ship from water.

**Figure 2 — A game in progress.** Misses are blue and marked `o`, hits are red and
marked `X`. Cells already fired at are disabled, so a second click does nothing.

**Figure 3 — A ship sunk.** The Destroyer's two cells turn a darker red, marked `#`,
and the status line falls to four ships and shows `Sunk!`. The brief requires hits to
be red; the darker shade is an addition separating a completed sinking from a ship
still afloat.

**Figure 4 — Victory.** The fleet is destroyed in eighty-nine shots and its sixteen
cells are now visible. The shapes can be counted on screen: one line of four, two of
three and three of two, none touching at an edge or a corner.

**Figure 5 — The victory message.** A dialog reports the end of the game and the
number of shots.

**Figure 6 — After Restart.** New Game clears the board and both counters, and a
fresh fleet is hidden in new positions.

## O'zbekcha (tekshirish uchun — hisobotga kirmaydi)

Quyidagi rasmlar ishlayotgan ilovadan, uni o'z tugmalari orqali boshqarib olingan.

**1-rasm — Yangi o'yin.** Yuz katakning hammasi ochilmagan; status qatorida o'q yo'q,
tegish yo'q, olti kema. Hech narsa kema turgan katakni suvdan ajratmaydi.

**2-rasm — Davom etayotgan o'yin.** Tegmaganlar ko'k va `o` belgili, tegganlar qizil
va `X` belgili. O'q uzilgan kataklar o'chirilgan, shuning uchun ikkinchi bosish hech
narsa qilmaydi.

**3-rasm — Cho'kkan kema.** Destroyer'ning ikki katagi to'q qizilga aylanadi, `#`
belgisi bilan, va status qatori to'rt kemaga tushib `Sunk!` ko'rsatadi. Brief
tegishlar qizil bo'lishini talab qiladi; to'qroq tus — qo'shimcha, u tugallangan
cho'kishni hali suzib yurgan kemadan ajratadi.

**4-rasm — G'alaba.** Flot 89 o'qda yo'q qilingan va uning o'n olti katagi endi
ko'rinadi. Shakllarni ekranda sanash mumkin: bitta to'rtlik chiziq, ikkita uchlik va
uchta ikkilik, hech biri yon yoki burchak bilan tegmaydi.

**5-rasm — G'alaba xabari.** Dialog o'yin tugaganini va o'qlar sonini bildiradi.

**6-rasm — Qayta boshlagandan keyin.** New Game taxta va ikkala hisoblagichni
tozalaydi, yangi flot yangi joylarda yashiriladi.
